Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the foundation and how to get involved.

What is SHIFA-BIRY FOUNDATION?

A community-focused foundation based in Mbale, Uganda, with interests in education, healthcare, child protection, rural development and sustainable livelihoods.

Can I volunteer or partner?

Yes, you can express interest by contacting us. Opportunities depend on current activities, suitability and available resources.

How do I make a donation?

Request verified donation instructions from the team before sending money.

Where can I find event dates?

Confirmed public dates will be shared on the Events page when available.
