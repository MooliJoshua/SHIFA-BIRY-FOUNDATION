Who We Are

A Bridge Between Hope and Opportunity

SHIFA-BIRY FOUNDATION is a non-profit organization committed to improving the lives of vulnerable communities through sustainable, community-led development.

Our work focuses on education, healthcare, rural development, child protection, poverty alleviation and community empowerment. We believe every person deserves the opportunity to live a healthy, safe and productive life.

We work directly with communities, local leaders, volunteers and development partners to identify real challenges and create practical solutions that can continue serving people for years.

  • Community-led programs
  • Sustainable solutions
  • Child-centred support
  • Transparent partnerships
  • Inclusive development
  • Long-term community impact
Explore Our Programs

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable children, families and communities through education, healthcare, child protection, economic opportunity and sustainable community development programs.

Our Vision

A Uganda where every child, family and community has access to the opportunities, resources and support needed to live with dignity, hope and independence.

Our Core Values

The Principles That Guide Our Work

Our values shape how we work with communities, volunteers, partners and supporters.

Compassion

We serve people with kindness, empathy, respect and a genuine desire to improve lives.

Integrity

We act honestly, responsibly and transparently in every program and partnership.

Community

We listen to communities and place local people at the centre of every decision.

Sustainability

We create practical solutions that communities can maintain and grow over time.

Our Approach

We Work With Communities, Not Just For Them

Lasting change happens when communities participate in identifying challenges, designing solutions and leading development.

01

Listen and Understand

We consult communities to understand their priorities, needs and existing strengths.

02

Design Together

Community members help shape solutions that are practical, inclusive and locally appropriate.

03

Implement and Empower

We provide resources, training and support while building local leadership and ownership.

04

Measure and Improve

We monitor progress, share lessons and improve programs based on evidence and community feedback.
